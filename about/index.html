About

We make tools for what’s already on the screen.

Small native tools for the devices you already use.

The ignored bits of the screen

We make native apps for interface corners most people skip. The notch, the screen edge, the gap between wallpaper and icons: those ignored spots are where a small tool can actually help.

That’s what we build: apps that sit in the quiet parts of Mac and iPhone, do one job well, and stay out of the way when you don’t need them.

What we ship

Here Wallpaper turns a place on the map into a lock screen wallpaper, and can run live on the Mac desktop. Sidefy keeps calendar, reminders, and feeds on the screen edge. Here Island puts Now Playing in the MacBook notch.

When something no longer fits, we move it to Unmaintained instead of pretending it’s still active.

A good place for a small tool

We’re a small independent studio. Some apps go to the App Store; others are open source on GitHub. We prefer local processing, no tracking, and interfaces that feel like they belong on the device.

If that sounds useful, start with the released apps, or find us on GitHub.
