About

We make tools for what’s already on the screen.

Who we are

We’re a small independent studio, founded August 15, 2025. We build native apps for Mac and iPhone that sit in quiet corners of the interface—the notch, the screen edge, the space around your wallpaper—and do one job without getting in the way.

What we ship

Here Wallpaper turns a place on the map into a lock screen wallpaper, and can run live on the Mac desktop. Sidefy keeps calendar, reminders, and feeds on the screen edge. Here Island puts Now Playing in the MacBook notch.

When something no longer fits, we move it to Unmaintained instead of pretending it’s still active.

Some apps go to the App Store; others are open source on GitHub. We prefer local processing, no tracking, and interfaces that feel like they belong on the device.

Start with the released apps, or find us on GitHub.
