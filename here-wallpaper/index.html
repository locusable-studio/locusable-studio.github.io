FAQ

Is Here Wallpaper free?

It’s free to download. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription.

What does Here Wallpaper do?

It turns places you care about into map wallpapers. You pick the place, theme, and layout.

What do I need to run it?

iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later. Once the Mac app ships, macOS 26 or later.

Does Here Wallpaper collect personal data?

No analytics, ads, or tracking. Location is used only when you tap the locate control.

What does Pro include?

iCloud sync, featured and custom themes, fonts, app icons, extra map layers, and the option to hide export credits.

Does it work on Mac?

Not yet. A dedicated Mac app is on the way, and one Pro subscription will cover iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
