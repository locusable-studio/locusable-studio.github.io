FAQ

Is Here Wallpaper free?

Here Wallpaper is free to download. Here Wallpaper Pro is available as a monthly or annual subscription.

What does Here Wallpaper Pro include?

Pro adds iCloud sync for favorites, Shortcuts support, more themes, fonts, and map layers, plus the option to hide export credits.

Where does the map data come from?

Maps use OpenStreetMap and related open projects, rendered with MapLibre.
