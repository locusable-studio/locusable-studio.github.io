FAQ

Is Here Wallpaper free?

It’s free to download. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription.

What does Here Wallpaper do?

It turns a map of a place you care about into a lock screen wallpaper. You pick the place, theme, typography, and layers, then export.

What do I need to run it?

iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later, or a Mac with macOS 26 or later.

Does Here Wallpaper collect personal data?

No analytics, ads, or tracking. Location is used only when you tap the locate control.

What does Pro include?

More themes, fonts, and map layers, plus the option to hide export credits.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. The Mac app renders the same maps live on the desktop. One Pro subscription covers iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
