FAQ

Is Here Wallpaper free?

It’s free to download. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription.

What do I need to run it?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later, iPad with iPadOS 26 or later, or a Mac with macOS 26 or later.

Does Here Wallpaper collect personal data?

No analytics, ads, or tracking. Location is used only when you tap the locate control.

What does Pro include?

More themes, fonts, and map layers, plus the option to hide export credits. Pro can also control major roads separately from smaller ones.
