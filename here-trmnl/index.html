Desktop mirror

A menu bar app with no Dock icon. It polls your display API and keeps the latest frame on the desktop.

Between wallpaper and icons

Fixed device size, pick a screen and corner. Clicks pass through. Manual refresh and launch at login.

FAQ

Is Here TRMNL free?

Yes. It’s free to download, and the source is on GitHub.

What do I need to run it?

macOS 26.0 or later, plus a LaraPaper-compatible base URL, device ID, and access token.

Does Here TRMNL collect personal data?

No. It just mirrors your own screen onto your own desktop.
