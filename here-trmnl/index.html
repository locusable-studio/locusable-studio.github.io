FAQ

Is Here TRMNL free?

Yes. It’s free to download, and the source is on GitHub.

What does Here TRMNL do?

It turns this Mac into a LaraPaper / TRMNL BYOS display: the current screen sits between the wallpaper and desktop icons, with clicks passing through.

What do I need to run it?

macOS 26.0 or later, plus a LaraPaper-compatible base URL, device ID, and access token.

Does Here TRMNL collect personal data?

No. It just mirrors your own screen onto your own desktop.

Where do I get it?
