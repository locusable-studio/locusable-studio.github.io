FAQ

Is Sidefy free?

There’s a free trial. The full version is a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store, with no subscription.

What do I need to run it?

macOS 14.0 or later, Intel or Apple Silicon.

Does Sidefy collect personal data?

Sidefy does not collect personal data or use analytics. Connected services and plugins handle requests under their own privacy policies.
