FAQ

Is Sidefy free?

There’s a free trial. The full version is a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store, with no subscription.

What does Sidefy do?

It puts Calendar, Reminders, GitHub, RSS, and plugin events into a sidebar on the edge of your Mac.

What do I need to run it?

macOS 14 or later.

Does Sidefy collect personal data?

No. Your data stays in iCloud; the app collects nothing.

What does Sidefy integrate with?

Calendar, Reminders, RSS and Atom, GitHub activity, and community or custom plugins.
