FAQ

Is Here HackerBa free?

Yes. It’s free and open source under the MIT License.

What does Here HackerBa do?

A Chrome extension that restyles Hacker News as a Tieba-like forum. Your HN account and content stay the same.

What do I need to run it?

Chrome, and an internet connection to news.ycombinator.com.

Does Here HackerBa collect personal data?

No. It restyles the page in your browser. Search queries go to the Algolia Hacker News API.

How do I install it?

Open Chrome’s extensions page, enable Developer mode, load the unpacked build, then open any HN page.
