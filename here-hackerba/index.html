FAQ

Is Here HackerBa free and open source?

Yes. Here HackerBa is free and open source under the MIT License.

What does Here HackerBa do?

Here HackerBa is a Chrome extension that restyles Hacker News as a Tieba-like forum while keeping HN content and accounts unchanged.

How do I install it?

Load the unpacked extension from the GitHub repository in Chrome’s extensions page, then visit any news.ycombinator.com page.
