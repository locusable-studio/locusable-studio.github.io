FAQ

Is Here HackerBa free?

Yes. It’s free and open source under the MIT License.

What does Here HackerBa do?

A Chrome extension that restyles Hacker News into a Tieba-like forum. Your content and account stay the same.

What do I need to run it?

Chrome, and an internet connection to news.ycombinator.com.

Does Here HackerBa collect personal data?

No. It only restyles the page. Nothing leaves your browser.

How do I install it?

Open Chrome’s extensions page, load the unpacked build, then open any HN page.
