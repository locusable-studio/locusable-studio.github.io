FAQ

Is Here Island free and open source?

Yes. Here Island is free and open source under the GNU General Public License v3.0.

What does Here Island do?

Here Island is a focused macOS notch media companion with album art, track details, playback controls, progress, and an optional real-time waveform.

What do I need to run it?

Here Island requires macOS 26.0 or later.
