FAQ

Is Here Island free?

Yes. It’s free and open source under GPL v3.

What does Here Island do?

It shows what’s playing in the notch: artwork, controls, progress, and an optional live waveform.

What do I need to run it?

macOS 26.0 or later.

Does Here Island collect personal data?

No. It only shows what’s playing on this Mac.
