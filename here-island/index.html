Here Island

Now Playing in the MacBook notch.

Download View on GitHub

Playing lives in the notch

Album art, title, artist, and progress stay in the notch. Optional real-time waveform while it plays.

Controls when you hover

Hover to expand playback controls — shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, and repeat, plus progress.

New track, quick peek

Title marquee on track change, so a new song shows up without hovering.

Now Playing or Apple Music

System-wide Now Playing by default, or follow Apple Music.

Lock screen card, optional

Optional media card with artwork, controls, and progress on the menu-bar display.

Out of screenshots and fullscreen

Hide during screenshots and recordings, or when an app is in native fullscreen.

Homebrew

Install with Homebrew. Trust the package first.

brew tap locusable-studio/tap
brew trust --cask locusable-studio/tap/here-island
brew install --cask here-island

FAQ

Is Here Island free?

Yes. It’s free and open source under GPL v3.

What do I need to run it?

macOS 26.0 or later.

Does Here Island collect personal data?

No. It only shows what’s playing on this Mac.
