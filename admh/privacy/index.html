Privacy Policy

ADMH · Locusable Studio · Effective November 19, 2026

This policy describes how ADMH handles microphone audio, memories, and requests to the AI provider you configure.

Microphone and on-device processing

When you enable All-day memory, ADMH uses the microphone to capture speech in the background. Speech transcription, speaker recognition, and search embeddings run on your device. Pending audio, memories, and downloaded models are stored on your device.

Your AI provider

ADMH sends relevant text to your configured AI provider for chat and background tasks such as topic grouping and daily summaries. Depending on the task, that text can include conversation transcripts, saved memories, and messages. Your provider handles these requests under its own privacy policy. ADMH stores your provider credentials privately on the device, encrypted with Android Keystore.

Model downloads

ADMH connects to third-party model hosts to download the files used for local processing. Those services handle download requests under their own privacy policies.
