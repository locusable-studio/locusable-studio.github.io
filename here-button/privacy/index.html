Privacy Policy

Here Button · Locusable Studio · Updated October 19, 2026

This policy covers Here Button and its product pages on locusable.com.

App data and Accessibility

Here Button uses macOS Accessibility permission to identify and trigger controls you choose in other apps. Pin names, app identifiers, window titles, control titles and descriptions, panel preferences, and trigger counts and timestamps are stored locally on your Mac. Window and control labels may contain information from the apps you pin. Here Button does not upload this data to Locusable Studio or include advertising or analytics tracking services.

You can remove pins in the app and revoke Accessibility permission in System Settings. Settings and bindings are saved in ~/Library/Application Support/HereButton/pins.json. Uninstalling the app may leave this file on your Mac; remove the HereButton folder to delete the saved data.

Website and support

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP addresses for delivery and security. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message and use them to respond to your request.

Purchases when sales open

Checkout is planned through Paddle. Paddle will process payment and billing information under its own privacy policy. We will receive the purchase email address, order details, and payment status needed to deliver licenses and handle support or refunds. We will not receive full payment card details.

Retention and your choices

We retain support correspondence and purchase records only as needed to provide support, manage licenses, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold about you. Required transaction records may need to be retained.
