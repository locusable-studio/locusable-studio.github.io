Privacy Policy

Here Button · Locusable Studio · Updated October 19, 2026

Here Button is a free macOS app made by Locusable Studio. This policy covers the app and its product pages on locusable.com. No account, payment, or license activation is required.

App data and Accessibility

Here Button uses macOS Accessibility permission to identify and trigger controls you choose in other apps. Pin names, app identifiers, window titles, control titles and descriptions, combinations, panel preferences, and trigger counts and timestamps are stored locally on your Mac. Window and control labels may contain information from the apps you pin. Here Button does not upload this data to Locusable Studio and does not include advertising or analytics tracking services.

You can remove pins and combinations in the app and revoke Accessibility permission in System Settings. Bindings and panel position are saved in ~/Library/Application Support/HereButton/pins.json; app preferences are also stored locally through macOS. Uninstalling the app may leave local data on your Mac. To remove saved bindings, quit the app and delete its HereButton folder from Application Support.

Updates and downloads

Here Button uses Sparkle to check for updates through a feed hosted on GitHub Releases. Update checks and installer downloads connect to GitHub, which may process technical request data such as your IP address. Your saved pins and control labels are not included in these requests. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its handling of request data.

Website and support

The website is delivered through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. These providers may process technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site. Their handling of this data is described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message and use them to respond to your request. Reports you submit to the public GitHub Issues page are publicly visible; avoid including private information from other apps in screenshots or recordings.

Retention and your choices

We retain support correspondence only as needed to respond to your request and provide support. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us. App bindings and preferences remain under your control on your Mac.
