Refund Policy

Here Button · Locusable Studio · Updated October 19, 2026

This policy will apply when Here Button sales open.

Refund eligibility

For purchases through Paddle, refund eligibility is determined by Paddle’s Refund Policy and applicable consumer protection law. Any statutory withdrawal or refund rights remain unaffected.

Paddle may consider discretionary refund requests submitted within 14 days of purchase. A request within that period does not guarantee approval.

How to request a refund

Use the support link in your purchase receipt or visit Paddle purchase support and select “Request refund”. If you contact us, include your order number and the purchase email address so we can help locate the transaction.

Product or delivery problems

For duplicate charges, missing delivery, or technical problems, contact us at the address below. We will help investigate the issue and assist with a refund request where appropriate.

Payment and processing

Paddle processes approved refunds, generally through the original payment method. The time for the refund to appear depends on the payment provider and your bank.

After a refund

After a full refund, your paid license ends and you must stop using that license.
